Critical Forensic Finding

When a multi-hop money trail routes funds through a closed circuit of entities and conduits, standard audits often overlook the transaction chain if no single 1-to-1 transfer appears illicit. In forensic finance, what actually moved is beneficial control: funds were deliberately layered through external intermediary conduits or unlinked VPAs to obscure the audit trail, extract conduit commission margins, and sanitize capital before cycling back to the originating network.
